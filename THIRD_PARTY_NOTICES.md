## Proprietary BLACKDARK IP (100% owned)

Application source in this repository (`*.py`, templates, configs) excluding
third-party packages above:

- **CVVD** — Cross-Venue Volume Discrepancy (`whale_tracker.py`)
- **SII** — Sector Inflow Index (`whale_tracker.py`)
- **Oracle scoring engine** (`ai_oracle.py`, `oracle_retrainer.py`)
- **77-type arbitrage taxonomy** (`arbitrage_catalog.py`, `arbitrage_engine.py`)
- **ML flywheel pipeline** (`ml/`, `ml_flywheel_scheduler.py`)
- **100-exchange universe registry** (`market_fetcher_hub.py`, `data/universe_registry.json`)
- **B2B institutional feed + WebSocket hub** (`b2b_websocket_hub.py`, `whale_tracker.py`)
- **Operational manifest & liquidity discovery** (`liquidity_discovery.py`)

Historical datasets (`data/`, parquet, SQLite) are **generated by BLACKDARK**
ingestion — not third-party code.
