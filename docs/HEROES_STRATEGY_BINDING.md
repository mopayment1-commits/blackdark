---

## Governing principle

Success is measured by depth of a few hero experiences that convert paying users and impress acquirers — not by feature count.

Test every change: **Does this strengthen one of the six heroes, or add a seventh button to an already crowded page?**

---

## Section A — Six Heroes (max polish priority)

1. **Opportunity Score + Explainability** — understand why in <5 seconds  
2. **Whale Intelligence + Market Radar** — one narrative sentence  
3. **Public Accuracy Ledger** — full public proof, no login  
4. **Portfolio AI** — portfolio risk in plain language  
5. **Single-Sentence Oracle** — one symbol → one decision sentence  
6. **Decision Certificate** — exportable, shareable proof per decision  

Internal engines (~250 items) feed these heroes quietly — never as separate retail pages.

Time allocation: **~60% heroes · ~30% quiet engines · ~10% real user feedback**.

Launch-ready when a new user grasps value in the first **60 seconds** without explanation.

## Section Z — Five ideas (approved for execution)

| # | Idea | Deepens | Implementation |
|---|------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | **Locked Predictions** | Decision Certificate + Public Ledger | Timed sealed forecasts before major events; reveal after |
| 2 | **Discipline Mirror** | Portfolio AI / behavioral learning | Private optional follow-up: did you follow the signal? Personal delta page |
| 3 | **Signal vs Noise Whale Classifier** | Whale Intelligence | Classify transfer vs real flow; cross-check Funding/OI hedges |
| 4 | **Emerging Fund Onboarding** | B2B + Evidence Pack | Fund path: DD export from Ledger + Certificate at 10–50M pricing posture |
| 5 | **Compliance Footer** | Anti-Hype / AI-washing readiness | Shared UI under every AI output: source · trust basis · disclaimer |

Glass Box Challenge remains the **primary launch narrative** (timing/announcement) built on Locked Predictions + public ledger — not a separate product surface.
