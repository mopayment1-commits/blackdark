## Market truth (research-backed, binding posture)

Big competitors (Nansen / Arkham / Glassnode) sell **labels**, not **proven decisions**. Independent reviews document label failures (e.g. “Smart Money” wallets that were ordinary bots). **No major platform publishes a full public audited accuracy ledger including misses.**

Market default = **Trust me**. BLACKDARK posture = **Prove it** via Public Accuracy Ledger + Decision Certificate.

**Dashboard Tourism** (documented): more tabs ≠ better outcomes. Our answer is Single-Sentence Oracle + Discipline Mirror — not more indicators.

**TradingView friction:** hard alert caps even on paid plans. Our competitive posture: generous in-app inbox without that throttle theater.

**Funds:** ~78% of crypto funds are sub-$50M and underserved by Kaiko-class tooling → Emerging Fund Terminal.

**Transfers ≠ Trades:** whale tools mislabel custody/collateral/hedged flows → Signal vs Noise classifier (Funding/OI cross-check).

**AI-washing enforcement (SEC/CFTC/FINRA):** Anti-Hype Compliance Footer on every AI surface — every claim must point to the Ledger.

## Governing principle

Success = depth of a few hero experiences that convert payers and impress acquirers — **not feature count**.

Test: *Does this strengthen one of the six heroes, or add a seventh button?*

Time split: **~60% heroes · ~30% quiet engines · ~10% real user feedback**.

Launch-ready when a new user grasps value in **60 seconds** without explanation.
